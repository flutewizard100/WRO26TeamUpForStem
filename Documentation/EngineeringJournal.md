## Car Base Itteration

### Version 1.0:

In our first attemp at making our own car our main structure we use a full 3D printed body where planed to mount a much larger motor and servo and then on top all of our sesors. This plan unfortunately did not work because our sensors would not fit and it was excessively bulky and incovinient.

### Version 1.1:

After our first versions we attempted to keep our main structure and move around some parts to make it smaller, stronger, and fit better to do this we added a flat top layer to mount the sensors. This helped solve some of the space issues for most of the sesors but the base was still far to high for the lidar to fit and be able to scan the walls efectivly.

### Version 2.1:

After our tryials with a 3D printed base we desided to change to use a premade base and make many major changes leading us to use the LaTrax Dessert Prerunner base and remove the casing and the original structural plate keep the original servo and motor. After more research on the part we realized we needed to replace them with new compenents that allowed for greater control.
